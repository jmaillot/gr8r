## Shipped work

### 65-05 fix — Subtask tree indent must ADD, not replace (`c356d8d`)
The shared recursive renderer emitted an inline `padding-left` that **replaced** each
surface's base padding: in the list view (`.subrow`, 64px base) a depth-1 child rendered
48px LEFT of its parent; in the drawer the first step was only +10px. The renderer now
emits an inline `--ind` custom property and every surface computes
`padding-left: calc(<base> + var(--ind))`. E2e asserts the child's real content box sits
strictly right of its parent's.
