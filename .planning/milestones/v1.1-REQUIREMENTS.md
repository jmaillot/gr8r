## Changed requirements

None. R1–R8 were validated as written; the milestone adjusted its *implementation* (the demo
workspace switch was removed rather than migrated, and the demo now presents one honest
workspace), not its requirements.

## Known gaps carried into the next milestone

The audit found no unsatisfied requirement. The remaining items are deferred hardening and
coverage, tracked in `.planning/milestones/v1.1-MILESTONE-AUDIT.md` (tech debt section) and in
the Active section of `.planning/PROJECT.md`:

- `sameOrigin` on the invite-accept route, admin link-only invites without a warning, the
  `firstWorkspace()` fallback and the guest-profile spoof (membership surface).
- The browser URL is write-only and no `popstate` is handled; eight client exports are orphaned.
- The recent-search redaction is substring-based, and a free-text mention of a hidden project
  name on a visible row is still served (a declared non-goal of row-association redaction).
- Eight UI-only steps of the end-to-end flow are still unproven by an automated test.
